Chapter 2: Going to the Mela

Q1. What transport do you take to travel within your city/town/village?
Answer: I take the bus, car, bike etc. to travel within my village.
Q2. How do you prepare yourself for a trip?
Answer: 1. I put clothes, slippers, water bottles etc. into the bag neatly. 2. I check my tickets and keep some food to eat. 3. I get ready on time.
Q3. What do you think was in the special bag that Nita and Radha’s family carried?
Answer: The special bag that Nita and Radha’s family carried may contain a water bottle, sanitizer, umbrella, and few edible items like fruits, biscuits etc.
Q4. Why should safety measures be followed when traveling by bus, car, or while riding a bicycle?
Answer: We must follow safety measures to be safe and to avoid any kind of accident.
Q5. What is the purpose of the ‘Lost and Found’ booth?
Answer: The ‘Lost and Found’ booth helps to report lost items or missing persons.
Q6. What is the role of the police dog?
Answer: A police dog helps in searching and tracking missing persons. It also detects suspicious objects like explosives.
Q7. Why are fire engines and ambulances present in a mela?
Answer: Fire engines and ambulances are present to keep people safe in a mela. They help in emergencies by putting out fires and giving medical help.
Q8. How can we care for our elders?
Answer: We can care for our elders by: Respecting them Spending time with them Helping in daily work Eating and playing with them.
Q9. What kinds of vehicles do we see around us?
Answer: We see many vehicles around us, such as bikes, cars, auto-rickshaws, buses, trains, and airplanes.
Q10. How can we ensure safety during travel?
Answer: We can ensure safety during travel by: Looking both sides before crossing the road. Crossing at the zebra crossing. Not playing on the road. Following traffic rules and signals. Wearing a seat belt and helmet. Not putting hands or head out of vehicles.

Chapter 3: Celebrating Festivals

Q1. Have you ever traveled on a bus?
Answer: Yes, once I traveled from Pathiar to Kangra by bus with my parents.
Q2. What type of trees and flowers did you see during your trip?
Answer: On my trip from Pathiar to Kangra, I saw a variety of beautiful trees and colorful flowers along the way.
Q3. What safety measures should you take while traveling?
Answer: While traveling, we should follow these safety rules: Sit properly facing forward. Keep bags under the seat. Keep head, hands, and arms inside. Talk quietly and follow the driver’s instructions.
Q4. What safety measures should you take while riding a bicycle and while walking on the road?
Answer: We should wear a helmet while riding a bicycle. We should follow traffic rules and signals. We should walk on the footpath. We should cross the road at a zebra crossing.
Q5. What special dresses do you wear during festivals?
Answer: I like to wear traditional dresses during festivals.
Q6. What are the different modes of transport used during travel?
Answer: Trains, buses, cars, scooters, aeroplanes, etc.
Q7. How can one be safe during travels?
Answer: 1.) Stay close to your parents or a trusted adult. 2.) Carry only necessary things and keep them safe. 3.) Remember your name and your parents’ phone number.
Q8. How do festivals and celebrations bring us together?
Answer: Festivals bring people together by sharing happiness and spending time together.
Q9. How do you spend time with your cousins and relatives?
Answer: I spend time by playing games with my cousins and relatives.
Q10. Fill in the Blanks.
1.) Chiya and Nonikaa live in a small village near the mountains. 2.) The bus went along the curved roads where the hills were covered with colourful flowers. 3.) Rishi and his family walked to his Mama’s house through a narrow mud road. 4.) The red flowers that bloom in spring are called buransh. 5.) Haakh is a kind of leafy vegetable eaten with hot rice.

Lesson 4: Getting to Know Plants

Q1. Write the names of trees that you can recognize.
Answer: I can recognize banyan, banana, mango, peepal and neem trees.
Q2. Write the names of some shrubs.
Answer: Tulsi, hibiscus, curry leaf, rose, bougainvillea and henna are examples of shrubs.
Q3. Write the names of some herbs that you have seen and where you have seen them.
Answer: I have seen coriander, mint, mustard and sugarcane. I have seen coriander and mint at my home and mustard and sugarcane in the fields.
Q4. Write the names of some climbers or creepers that you have seen and where you have seen them.
Answer: I have seen jasmine and money plant at my home and cucumber, bitter gourd, muskmelon and pumpkin in the fields.
Q5. What statements can we make about leaves?
Answer: Plants have different types of leaves that differ in colour, shape, size, texture and smell.
Q6. What are the parts of a plant?
Answer: The root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit and seed are the different parts of a plant.
Q7. Did you notice any other animals, birds and insects on the plant?
Answer: I saw squirrels, crows and beetles on the tree.
Q8. What were they doing?
Answer: The squirrel was eating a fruit from the tree, the crow was making strange noises and the beetles were sitting on the leaves.
Q9. What would happen if there were no plants?
Answer: Plants give us food, oxygen, clothes, wood, medicines and shelter for many animals and birds. Without plants, life on Earth would not be possible.
Q10. How does the root help a plant to grow?
Answer: Roots help a plant grow by holding it firmly in the soil and absorbing water and minerals needed for its growth.
Q11. What is the role of the stem?
Answer: The stem transports water and minerals from the roots to other parts of the plant. It also provides support to leaves and branches.
Q12. Describe your favorite plant. Why is it your favorite?
Answer: Rose is my favorite plant because it has beautiful flowers of many colours.

Lesson 5: Plants and Animals Live Together

Q1. Why do animals choose to live near plants?
Answer: Animals live near plants because they get food, shelter and protection from them.
Q2. What small things can you see in the soil?
Answer: We can see small stones, grass, leaves, insects and twigs in the soil.
Q3. Where do new plants and animals come from during monsoons?
Answer: New plants and animals come from seeds, eggs and dormant stages because rain helps them grow.
Q4. Which animals can be seen around plants?
Answer: Squirrels, monkeys, rabbits, butterflies and insects can be seen around plants.
Q5. Which birds can be seen around plants?
Answer: Sparrows, parrots and cuckoos can be seen around plants.
Q6. How do animals, birds and insects depend on plants?
Answer: Animals, birds and insects depend on plants for food, shelter and protection.
Q7. What is soil made of?
Answer: Soil is made of small rock pieces, old leaves, roots, stems and dead insects.
Q8. Name two animals you observed near plants.
Answer: Butterfly and monkey.
Q9. Why do butterflies stay near plants?
Answer: Butterflies stay near plants to collect nectar.
Q10. Why do monkeys stay near plants?
Answer: Monkeys stay near plants to eat fruits.
Q11. When do birds make more sounds?
Answer: Birds make more sounds in the early morning and evening.
Q12. Write the life cycle of a butterfly in order.
Answer: Caterpillar stopped eating → Caterpillar formed a cocoon → It came out of the cocoon → It became a butterfly → It flew away gently.
